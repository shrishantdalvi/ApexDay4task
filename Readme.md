# Apex Lark – Day 04: Business Insights Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing retail business data using **Microsoft Excel**. The objective was to create an interactive dashboard using PivotTables, PivotCharts, KPI cards, and slicers to identify important business trends and generate actionable insights.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables
- PivotCharts
- Excel Slicers
- Data Analysis and Visualization

## Tasks Completed

### 1. PivotTable Analysis
Created PivotTables to analyze:
- Total Sales by Region
- Profit by Category
- Total Sales by Product Category
- Quantity Sold by Product
- Monthly Sales Performance
- Top 10 Customers by Sales

### 2. PivotCharts
Created interactive charts to represent:
- Sales by Region – Column Chart
- Monthly Sales Trend – Line Chart
- Profit by Category – Bar Chart
- Sales Distribution by Category – Pie Chart

### 3. Interactive Dashboard
Designed a unified Excel dashboard featuring:
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Interactive slicers for Region, Product Category, and Month
- Connected PivotTables and PivotCharts for dynamic filtering

## Key Business Insights
- **Top Revenue Region:** West
- **Best Performing Category:** Office Supplies
- **Highest Sales Month:** December 2023
- **Top Customer by Sales:** Sean Miller

The analysis identified variations in monthly sales, with December 2023 recording the highest sales during the analyzed period.

## Business Recommendations
1. **Regional Growth:** Apply successful strategies from the West region to improve performance in other regions.
2. **Category Optimization:** Maintain effective inventory planning for Office Supplies and explore suitable product bundles.
3. **Customer & Seasonal Planning:** Prepare for higher December demand and use personalized offers to retain valuable customers.

## Project Deliverables
- Excel workbook containing PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Interactive business dashboard
- Business Insights Report (PDF)

## Learning Outcomes
- Learned to create and customize PivotTables and PivotCharts.
- Practiced analyzing sales, profit, quantity, and customer data.
- Learned to connect slicers with multiple PivotTables.
- Developed an interactive Excel dashboard.
- Improved business analysis and data-driven decision-making skills.
